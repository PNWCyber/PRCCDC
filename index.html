01 The exercise loop

A working business. An active adversary.

Collegiate teams manage a realistic organization while a professional Red Team applies pressure. Technical skill matters. So do judgment, teamwork and communication.

Inside the competition / An illustrative story

The business can’t stop.
Neither can your team.

You inherit a company’s network. Customers still need its website. Staff still need their files. Then an unusual burst of sign-in attempts appears—and a new business request arrives.

Who investigates? Who keeps services running? Who explains the decision? That balance of technical work, teamwork, and communication is the point.

Fictional example for this design proof. Not the announced 2027 scenario or live competition data.
