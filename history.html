Pacific Rim Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition

Competition history

From its first competition to national championship runs, PRCCDC has given generations of students a place to practice cybersecurity as a team.

Selected milestones from university and participant reports. This is a growing archive, not a complete list of annual results.

2008

The Pacific Rim competition begins.

Founded by Barbara Endicott-Popovsky, PRCCDC begins in 2008. Idaho State University and the University of Washington co-host the inaugural regional event in Redmond.

University of Washington · founding account · Idaho State’s 2008 co-host report

2011

From regional champions to national champions.

The University of Washington wins the Pacific Rim regional at Highline Community College, then earns its first national CCDC championship.

University of Washington · 2011

2012

A repeat on the national stage.

UW repeats as regional and national champion. Highline Community College hosts the fifth Pacific Rim competition.

University of Washington · 2012

2014

Western earns its first regional title.

Western Washington University wins PRCCDC at Highline Community College and qualifies for nationals for the first time.

Western Washington University · 2014

2018

The challenge reaches industrial systems.

UW wins a 12-team regional at Highline College. The year’s scenario includes industrial SCADA systems alongside the business services teams must maintain.

UW Allen School · 2018

2020

The region adapts to an online event.

After COVID-19 cancels the in-person regional, Pacific Rim teams take part in the National Wildcard Event online on April 11.

OSU Security Club · participant report

2024

Oregon State makes it three in a row.

Oregon State University wins its third consecutive Pacific Rim title, continuing the run that began in 2022.

OSU Security Club · 2024

2026

Western reaches the national podium.

WWU wins the 13-school regional field at South Puget Sound Community College, then finishes third nationally—its first national podium finish.

Western Washington University · 2026
